# StoreTrak – Smart Shop Management & POS

StoreTrak is a shop management and Point of Sale (POS) system designed to simplify daily business operations. It brings billing, inventory, supplier management, udhaar tracking, and profit reporting together in one place.

## 🌐 Live Demo

[Visit StoreTrak](https://storetrak.vercel.app/)

## ✨ Features

- **Billing & POS:** Manage customer transactions and generate bills.
- **Inventory Management:** Track stock and manage products.
- **Supplier Management:** Keep supplier information organized.
- **Udhaar Management:** Track credit transactions and outstanding payments.
- **Profit Reports:** Monitor business performance and profits.
- **Real-Time Updates:** Keep shop operations connected and up to date.
